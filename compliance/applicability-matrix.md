## Beyond the skill

The skill stops on these points and refers to a professional:

- **Prohibited AI practice suspected** (not ruled out: no answer for ai.emotion_or_biometric). Stop work on the feature. Emotion inference and biometric categorisation are among the practices Article 5 of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 prohibits in some contexts. Do not build it before a lawyer has confirmed in writing that the use is allowed. The same stop applies to any other practice of Article 5 the agent notices in the code or the request, whatever the profile says.
- **Possible high-risk AI area** (not ruled out: no answer for ai.decisions_about_people). An AI system that takes or prepares decisions about persons may fall in an area of Annex III of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689. The high-risk regime is outside this skill: consult a lawyer who practises AI regulation before the feature goes live.
- **Health data or other special categories** (not ruled out: no answer for data.health_data; no answer for data.special_categories). Processing data of Article 9 GDPR needs an exception, and health data often brings sector rules on hosting and security that this skill does not cover. Consult the data protection officer or a data protection lawyer before collecting anything.
- **Audience includes minors** (not ruled out: no answer for audience.includes_minors). Consent of children, age checks, profiling and advertising towards minors need a legal analysis this skill does not replace. Consult a data protection professional.
- **Regulated sector** (not ruled out: no answer for operator.regulated_sector). Sector rules and a sector authority apply on top of what this matrix lists. Consult the compliance function or a lawyer of the sector.

Consult a qualified professional (lawyer, data protection officer, accessibility auditor) for every case listed under "Beyond the skill" and before relying on this matrix. See references/scoping/out-of-scope.md.
